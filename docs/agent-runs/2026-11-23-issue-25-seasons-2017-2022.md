## Objective and authorized scope

Build the five development seasons with the validated pilot pipeline:
- pin the inputs;
- run `check` and `evidence`;
- report accepted, quarantined and unresolved counts with causes;
- extend identity and chronology checks across seasons;
- record weaker-verification strata;
- publish aggregate coverage;
- benchmark runtime and memory.

Constraints:
- no downloads;
- never open 2025–26 or 2026–27 payloads;
- keep the thresholds;
- keep the 2022–23 rebuild row-identical;
- record a failing season as unavailable.

## Work completed

- **`pilot_sources.py`:** Pins for starting years 2017 to 2021 were generated from the three #51 register CSVs, not typed by hand. `has_livedata(year)` reports whether the pinned archive holds `cdnnba`. Snapshots for 2017 to 2019 omit it, giving 10 inputs and 6 evidence files. The refusal of 2025 and later is unchanged.
- **`pilot_inputs.cross_feeds` and `pilot._season_games`:** liveData is optional. A game without liveData evidence gets the `no_livedata` stratum. Games absent from V2 now receive their cross-feed record, which changes no 2022–23 row.
- **`pilot_evidence.py`:** Stages an empty liveData table when the feed is absent. Skips the liveData starter check for games without it and counts them as `livedata_unavailable`. Adds a V3 versus data.nba period comparison. `no_livedata` joins `LOWER_TRUST`.

  - game IDs are unique and encode the season and type;
  - seasons do not overlap;

## Key decisions and discoveries

- **Falsification applied as written.** 2017–18, 2018–19 and 2021–22 miss an M1 threshold and are recorded as unavailable. 2018–19 and 2021–22 fail only the 95% overtime-stratum floor, at 58 of 67 and 55 of 58. Either reading of the floor, per season type or pooled, fails them. The thresholds were not changed.
- **2017–18.** 158 of 178 regular-season quarantines fall from October to December 2017, mostly `EventOrderError` (99) and cross-clock moves (65). The unresolved share is 0.39%. Counted possessions equal pbpstats.com's in only 871 of 1,132 games. This points to early-season V2 order defects (Inferred). It was not investigated at the event level.
- **No liveData (ADR-0020).** 2019–20 meets M1 anyway. Its three V3 period-score quarantines are exactly the three games where V3 and data.nba disagree, so ADR-0011 could likely recover them with liveData (Inferred).
- **2019–20.** It is missing three V2 games, `0021901316` to `0021901318`, as the #51 audit found. They are quarantined as `missing_v2_game`. The restart site games all have box home flags.
- **ADR numbering.** The lead reserved ADR-0020 and EXP-0009, because ADR-0018 and ADR-0019 and EXP-0008 are taken.

## Blockers and remaining risks

- **#26 window.** Under the current thresholds, the M1 seasons from 2017–18 to 2022–23 are 2019–20, 2020–21 and 2022–23, which are not contiguous. #26 needs a maintainer decision.
- **Stratum floor.** The 95% stratum floor on small overtime denominators decides two seasons. This is a policy question, not a data defect.
- **`no_livedata` sensitivity.** The stratum covers whole seasons. If a single-season RAPM sensitivity run drops it, nothing is left. The current RAPM baseline is 2022–23-specific, so it does not yet hit this.
- **Holdout boundary.** No 2025–26 or 2026–27 payload was opened or listed. Audit files were checked by exact expected name only.
