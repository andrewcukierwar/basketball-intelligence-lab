## Objective and scope

Probe one representative season of the earliest feed era on the default path (no ADR-0023 order repair): pin, capture from local copies, build, `check`, `evidence`, `season_quality`, and a canonical row set only if the ADR-0022 hard gates pass. Data only, no model, no network, no 2025–26 or later files, nothing under `data/` committed.

## Work completed

- **Season choice:** 2002–03. It is the only season of the three with no register exception (2001–02 has 3 regular games with incomplete V3 period scores), and its 1,189-game regular season (29 teams) matches the register.
- **Pin:** 2002 added to `pilot_sources.py` (archives, box, Bentes) from the #51 register CSVs. The season-list test now expects 2002. Commit `0a258cd`.
- **Capture:** `--from-local` from `data/raw/historical-feed-audit/2026-11-23`, 8 inputs and 4 evidence files verified by size and SHA-256. Every payload was present.
- **Build:** `issue-105-v1`, 38 s, 0.74 GB. `check` 26 of 26. `evidence` run. `season_quality` into `data/processed/quality/issue-105-v1`.
- **Docs:** `docs/data-pilot.md` (probe section, matrix row, pin list), `docs/research/backfill-feasibility.md` (results and batch 5 row).
