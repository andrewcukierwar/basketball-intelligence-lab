## Decision

ADR-0019 is Accepted on the maintainer's principle: exclude only verified pseudo-possessions with no live-ball opportunity, keep run-outs, and never condition eligibility on later actions or outcomes. A first revision made `period_end` starts canonical. Independent review showed that the audit behind it measured duration over the possession's own events, which is zero by construction. The corrected audit shows all 962 accepted `period_end` starts begin after a change of control with 3 to 24 s on the clock, while the pilot's 1,567 excluded starts all have under 3 s. So **canonical = the pilot-eligible set = `issue-59-v1` model rows**, and the registered #23 and #24 results stand.

## Results

On canonical held-out rows (136,730), fits on `period_end_start` and `no_action` rows score 1.74 (1.01 to 2.42) and 1.86 (1.10 to 2.57) MSE better than the canonical fit, and `no_action` beats `period_end_start` by 0.12 (0.07 to 0.18). The earlier sensitivity runs are kept: the time-block gain falls from −25.7 to about −5 when run-outs are dropped. These describe the sample and do not select the rule.
