# Availability-aware pregame minutes benchmark

Proposed registration for [#170](https://github.com/andrewcukierwar/basketball-intelligence-lab/issues/170). Test one availability adjustment using the existing [#55](https://github.com/andrewcukierwar/basketball-intelligence-lab/issues/55) report and schedule receipts. Hold outcomes and ratings fixed across both arms. At design time, the authorized local archive contains one captured report date. A prospective benchmark starts only after the exact inputs, candidate and fresh schedule cohort have been pinned and independently reviewed.

## Inputs, cutoff and coverage

- Use NBA-attributed private inputs under the project source ledger. Require a valid receipt for the exact PDF bytes, known capture and publication times, a matching schedule snapshot and a verified official game/team/player crosswalk. The local collector pins a game crosswalk and the report PDF. A candidate player-status parser still needs to retain per-row provenance and pass a comparison on exposed development reports. Quality-check a whole team submission, including explicit “not yet submitted” and malformed or ambiguous rows, before interpreting absent player entries.
- Declare forecast origin at scheduled tip minus 30 minutes using the schedule visible at that cutoff. Use the last valid report known by that origin and freeze one forecast no later than scheduled tip minus 25 minutes. Require its PDF publication and capture to be less than 24 hours old, and the exact schedule and player/team mapping to be available by the same cutoff. Reject reports for stale schedule revisions, including postponed and moved games without an unambiguous replacement keyed to the official game ID. The scorer must independently verify origin ≤ generation ≤ freeze < actual tip, and confirm the receipt meets the registered scheduled-tip freeze deadline. Late or missing freeze receipts are coverage failures even when the game tipped later. If actual tip, report, or a required crosswalk is missing, record the particular reason against the full expected denominator.
- Preserve #39's existing same-season last-ten-team-games lookback and previous-season fallback. Read only earlier official played dates with as-of availability evidence. Resolve roster/team revisions independently at each cutoff. Accept an eligible player on the last evidenced team until a verified transaction changes it. Missing box lines alone do not establish departure. A known trade removes the player from the old team; the new team seeds him from prior earlier minutes on his previous team where the history can be resolved. A rookie or new signing without an earlier minute history starts at zero in this first candidate and counts in the cold-player coverage report. No realized lineup or minutes can establish pregame membership.

## Fixed comparator and adjustment

Baseline is #39's last-ten-earlier-games forecast with the registered roster eligibility, including the same trades, cold-seed policy and previous-season fallback. Also reproduce the untouched #39 baseline on eligible historical development data as a lineage check. The comparison between the two prospective arms changes only injury-report weights. For a usable team submission, use this fixed mapping.

| Report status | Multiplier |
| --- | ---: |
| Available or player unlisted in a verified complete team submission | 1.00 |
| Probable | 0.85 |
| Questionable | 0.50 |
| Doubtful | 0.25 |
| Out | 0.00 |

These values define the one candidate as a rule, with no fitted injury probabilities. Conflicting or unrecognized statuses for a roster player, missing team submission, stale report or ambiguous crosswalk trigger the matched baseline for that team and a logged coverage failure. When eligible positive weights remain, renormalize to 240 regulation team minutes in both arms. An all-zero adjusted projection also falls back and is reported. Without a usable as-of roster or positive baseline weight, mark both arms unavailable and count the game against the scheduled denominator. Keep the same independently pinned ratings, cold rating priors, home effect, calibration and win-probability mapping in both arms. Drop any adjustment learned after the forecast exists. Late scratches and returns are outcomes and may be categorized after scoring. Oracle minutes use observed minutes normalized to 240, and are a separate diagnostic only.

## Prospective registration and scoring

Before selecting a new denominator, test the parser, roster transitions, earlier-cutoff filtering, immutable publication and exact baseline parity on public development or synthetic cases. Require a separate scorer, an independently timestamped schedule denominator and predictions before outcomes. Register eight consecutive NBA game weeks with at least 200 expected regular-season games and pin the first and last date and included game IDs. The schedule determines the denominator regardless of capture coverage. A scorer may honor only the predeclared schedule correction rules. Keep #44's existing box-only capture independent. Run only within authorized private capture and retention scope.

Primary outcome is player-minute MAE aggregated first per team-game and then over matched games. The scorer counts predicted roster players who did not play as zero and actual participants omitted by the prediction as zero predicted, using the union of the two forecasts and the scorer's official participating player IDs for a common denominator. The scorer uses regulation-normalized actual minutes for comparability across overtime. Report total minute mass and the fractions assigned to actual participants, missed late scratches, coverage and fallback by team, week, status, trade/cold cohort and injury burden. All games with both team receipts and an eligible baseline remain in an intention-to-forecast comparison, including candidate fallback. Show the fully availability-covered games as a separate diagnostic and report misses over the full scheduled denominator.

Secondary outcomes are paired team margin RMSE and game win log loss. Report all three deltas with paired week-block bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, seed 170, reusing sampled weeks across arms), and paired game-cluster intervals as sensitivity. Require at least 90% of the expected games with both teams' usable availability inputs and no fewer than eight nonempty weeks for the primary decision. Recommend the candidate only if the 95% week-block interval for its minute-MAE difference is below zero and neither team metric has a positive point-estimate difference. The team outcomes remain secondary and the point-estimate safeguard is a conservative decision rule. If coverage or precision is inadequate, keep the baseline and report the unavailable or inconclusive result.
