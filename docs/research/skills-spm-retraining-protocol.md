# Retraining SPM on as-of Estimated Skills

This development experiment addresses [#148](https://github.com/andrewcukierwar/basketball-intelligence-lab/issues/148). It tests whether refitting the SPM on the same Skills representation it receives at inference improves the prior-informed Expected Impact predictions. The #125 applied-Skills and raw-box variants are frozen comparators. The already examined 2023–25 folds supply retrospective development evidence. The 2025–26 holdout and EPM values are outside the input allowlist.

## Inputs and fitting

- Use the contiguous #28 target reference, its manifest and calendar, and the five `K365` versions closing 2018–19 through 2022–23. Keep the exact target labels and seasons for each frozen origin. For a label in target season `S`, compute features at the day after that season's pinned final game. The feature cutoff must be at or before the label's frozen origin. This keeps later seasons out of older label rows when later frozen origins update their labels.
- Use `bil.features.skill_bundle` for both calibration and inference. For season `S`, fit on the three preceding seasons using #125's existing settings, seeds, clock choice, support floor and provenance checks. Use the existing #125 archived bundle for 2021–24, through the manifest SHA-256 `97e083eae593e922a65e36412adc9b1f218a38a34e1dba318c0056f68e5bf529`. Fit bundles for 2018–20 with the same recipe. Use the full registered lookback. Every training and opening snapshot must pass `check_bundle` and the pinned source-view check in `bundle_values`.
- Use #30's verified box-log pins for 2015–16, 2016–17 and 2018–23, #36's pins for 2023–25, and the #25 box-log snapshot `issue-25-v1` for 2017–18 with digest `b7df4c826d425723db81de1af117fb3cd533d0c7df68e1612b59fd81bcd9fc60`. Include 2017–18 solely as an earlier box-log history input. Its possession data remains outside the canonical cohort under ADR-0022. All source captures postdate historical cutoffs, so the evidence is `retrospective_capture_bound`.
- Keep the #120 joint ≥250 possessions per side and positive-minute target-season box-history cohort. Keep the side weights `n / (n + 1000)`, the treatment of one-sided labels, weighted transforms and ridge 0.05. Freeze the Full, Zero, Mean and One-stat candidates at each origin. Record the eligible and missed target keys as counts, exposure rule, feature cutoff and bundle ID by target season, source pins, target hashes and fitted transforms. Refuse if any required season lacks support or a parent is late.

## K0 diagnostic

Use #120 §4's contiguous K0 scoring labels, season-opening cutoffs, minimum exposure, player-cluster intervals and slices. The features at each opening use the #125 history window and its cold fallback, consistent with the retrained features. Minutes and shot-volume slices use observed box history from that same window, including raw `fga_per_36` for shot volume. Record each opening cutoff and its SPM and Skills fit IDs. Compare Full with Zero and One-stat on the four 2019–23 openings and report RMSE, MAE and bias by side and total. Keep the K0 scorer behind the fit freeze. K0 measures agreement with the registered surrogate target. It is a separate diagnostic from lineup outcome prediction.

## Matched Expected Impact comparison

- Use the fit closing 2020–21 for EI season 2021–22, the 2021–22 close for 2022–23, and the 2022–23 close for both outer seasons. Verify fit eligibility at each origin. Use the #125 box streams, row sets, context, `W2` window, playoffs and regular-season blocks, and fixed 8,000/8,000 penalties.
- Read the archived #125 predicted arrays `ei_raw` and `ei` through the pinned manifest. Check complete ordered equality on `(block, game_id, possession_seq)` and finite predictions before scoring any fold. Reuse the per-block, season and role scoring with paired game- and week-cluster intervals. Compare retrained EI to both archived candidates. Report the phase-specific score and digest of every prediction file, and verify the input and fit identities in the output manifest.
- The primary contrast is retrained versus raw-box EI on the pooled 2023–25 regular season. Apply the #125 rule requiring the paired 95% game interval to exclude zero and the corresponding direction in both outer seasons. The applied-Skills contrast, development folds and playoffs are descriptive. Report a negative or inconclusive result and retain the raw-box baseline absent improvement. A favorable result nominates a later candidate for independent validation. The experiment does not promote any rating artifacts.
