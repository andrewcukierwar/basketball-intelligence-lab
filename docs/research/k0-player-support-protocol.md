# K0 agreement and individual player support

This development-only diagnostic addresses [#137](https://github.com/andrewcukierwar/basketball-intelligence-lab/issues/137). Test whether player ratings that agree with a shrinkage target also replicate on a separate schedule block and predict later outcomes. Preserve the published #28, #30 and #121 scores and the provisional raw-box prior. Use only pinned #28 contiguous regular-season possessions from 2019–20 through 2022–23. These development seasons have prior exposure. No reserved season or EPM data enters this experiment.

## Schedule partitions and candidate fits

Within each season, order distinct played dates. The first half of dates supplies calibration, the next quarter supplies the repeat block, and the final quarter supplies the held-out outcome block. Use integer floor for both cut positions. Keep every game intact. Split calibration games into candidate and proxy subsets using parity of SHA-256 of `issue-137-v1:<game ID>`. Report dates, games, possessions and input digests by subset. Withhold the repeat and outcome responses when fitting the candidate or proxy.

Use #28's one-season joint RAPM objective, unit weights and unpenalized pre-possession context. Fit candidate ridge pairs `(1,000, 1,000)`, `(4,000, 4,000)`, `(16,000, 16,000)`, `(64,000, 64,000)` and the context-only zero-player benchmark on the candidate subset. The proxy and the independent repeat estimate both use `(16,000, 16,000)`, on their own subsets. These fixed penalties span different amounts of shrinkage. The proxy is a K0 analogue with disjoint games and explicitly fixed penalties. The published #28 frozen contiguous `evaluation_K0` uses the full season and enters only a separate descriptive agreement column after prediction freeze.

## Player ratings and lineup support

- Report offensive and defensive player agreement separately. Use matched players with at least 250 possessions in that role in each fitted subset. On each matched cohort, center each candidate, proxy and repeat rating by its candidate-subset exposure. Report equal-player RMSE and the exposure-weighted standard deviation of the centered coefficients. Partition exposure at 1,000 candidate possessions. Report cold players separately on later outcomes, assigning unseen player coefficients zero.
- For each player and role, report the largest Jaccard overlap with an exposed same-role teammate in candidate possessions. Shared possessions supply the intersection. Treat overlap at or above 0.50 as high and report agreement on both sides of that threshold. This measures a specific source of collinearity. Other players and context can still make a contrast weak.
- For the individual contrast, hold the other four teammates and the five opponents fixed within a season, and compare the two players who occupied the remaining slot. A matched contrast requires both players to appear in the same nine-player configuration in at least two games and 15 possessions each in both the candidate and outcome blocks. Report coverage and matched contrast RMSE against the held-out difference in mean points per 100 after subtracting the frozen candidate context-only prediction. Reverse the outcome sign for defense. Report the estimate as an observational contrast. If common support is insufficient, record unavailable coverage.

## Outcomes and decision

Freeze model predictions and their digests before scoring. Score possession MSE and paired differences to the 16,000 ridge candidate on the same regular-season rows, with game- and week-cluster intervals, separately by season and pooled. Report both all-ten candidate exposure at least 250 and any cold-player cohorts. For whole-lineup MSE, repeat the paired estimate after removing every held-out game involving each franchise in turn. Report the minimum and maximum of those estimates. For ratings, report separate season results and pooled comparisons by player-cluster resampling to expose repeated-player dependence.

A candidate's agreement ordering can support a player claim only where the proxy ordering persists in the disjoint repeat block and in supported held-out contrasts for that role. Report reversals, unresolved contrasts and missing support. Aggregate lineup forecast accuracy supplies evidence about the lineup prediction. Its interpretation should remain at lineup level when the individual contrast lacks support. These diagnostics do not promote or replace the published K0, SPM or EI artifacts.
