# Development protocol for alternate RAPM lineup exposure

**Status:** Frozen before fitting or profiling private rows. [Issue #110](https://github.com/andrewcukierwar/basketball-intelligence-lab/issues/110) evaluates recovery of multiple-lineup possessions as a bounded research proposal. The canonical [stable-lineup contract](rapm-historical-rows.md) remains the reference. This is a retrospective replay using realized lineups and the approved capture-bound inputs.

## Data and comparisons

Use only 2019–20 through 2022–23, pinned to their canonical `issue-93-v1` row sets and the corresponding pilot builds in the [ADR-0025 protocol](rapm-multiseason-protocol.md#frozen-inputs). Verify manifest and used table digests. The 730-day window at the first development origin begins in October 2019, so earlier seasons cannot contribute. Score only 2021–22 and 2022–23. Do not read 2023–24, 2024–25 or the 2025–26 confirmatory holdout. The runner accepts a processed-data directory and only constructs paths for the four named development seasons. Keep all source and row-level data outside version control. NBA source data are attributed to NBA.com.

Keep ADR-0025 fixed for all fits: W2 with Eastern played dates aged 1 through 730 days, one unit of weight per training possession, zero prior, an unpenalized intercept, and penalties of 4,000 offense and 8,000 defense. Use its R1–R4 and P refit schedule and keep whole games in one block. Each scored row must be from a game on or after its model cutoff. The canonical fit uses only its stable-lineup rows. The following two **training** alternatives each add the otherwise admissible `multiple_lineups` possessions and retain the same stable rows.

| Representation | Estimand and exposure |
| --- | --- |
| Start lineup | Treat the first actual lineup pair overlapping the half-open event interval as the lineup at entry. This estimates whole-possession scoring associated with the entry pair, including what happens after it changes. Each entrant in that pair receives a full opportunity in their role. |
| Clock-fractional lineup | Estimate whole-possession scoring from the weighted mean of the actual lineup states during the possession. Each intersecting stint receives its fraction of the reconciled possession clock, including the pre-first-event interval from the preceding event if in-period. Clamp the start to the period start and end at the final included event. Positive-clock possessions give zero weight to same-clock pieces with no elapsed time. When the entire possession has no elapsed time, divide one opportunity equally among the sequence-intersecting stint pieces. Report the fallback share. |

The fractional fit has one observation and one response per possession. Its signed player design contains fractional role exposures. It gives no separate scoring target or independent residual to a fragment. Both alternatives preserve one offensive opportunity and five player-opportunities per role per possession. The entire signed `control_possession` point sum belongs to the canonical possession, including zero totals. Match the offense team, check the assigned points against the source score-allocation ledger, and verify the partition with parser-excluded and unresolved possessions. Dead-ball and unresolved allocations never enter any model. The official credited lineup does not determine exposure. The runner must stop on an inconsistent source, date, score or exposure check rather than silently omitting the affected rows.

Compare all three fits on **exactly the same canonical stable-lineup test possessions**. Those are the common cohort for which a frozen baseline prediction is defined and both alternative designs reduce to the same binary lineups. No candidate is scored on the additional training rows. Report regular-season pooled and per-season MSE differences (candidate minus reference), game-clustered percentile intervals with 2,000 resamples and seed 2023, and team-game summed-control-point RMSE. Report each regular-season refit block separately. Report pooled playoff comparisons separately and treat them as a check. Reproduce the reference's development row coverage and MSE against the existing ADR-0025 result before interpreting candidates. An unseen player has zero estimated effect. Training counts and average exposure per fit are recorded because keeping raw penalty values while adding opportunities changes shrinkage relative to exposure.

## Exclusion profile

On the two scored development seasons, report regular and playoff group counts and scoring rates by disposition. Use the pre-possession scoreboard and clock immediately before the first included event. Report excluded shares by signed score-state bands based on offense minus defense (≤−10, −9 to −3, −2 to +2, +3 to +9, ≥+10), period (1–4 and overtime), and clock (0–24 s, >24–120 s, >120–360 s, >360 s). For the excluded group, report offense-only, defense-only and both-side lineup changes, two versus three-or-more lineup pairs, possessions containing zero-time pieces, and total-zero-clock fallback use.

Characterize team exposure using the per-season/team/role fraction of accepted possessions omitted by the stable condition. Characterize player exposure using the share of accepted fractional player exposure omitted by the stable condition, by role and among player/season/role cells with at least 200 accepted equivalent opportunities. Report the 10th percentile, median and 90th percentile and group sizes without identifiers.

## Decision rule

Propose further validation of a candidate only if its pooled regular-season MSE improvement is at least one squared-rate unit, its game-clustered interval excludes zero in the favorable direction, it improves both regular seasons individually, its pooled regular-season team-game RMSE does not increase, and the playoff interval does not indicate clear harm. Otherwise recommend retaining the stable-lineup restriction for the current reference. A favorable development result would support a registered follow-up on a separately approved evaluation cohort. Even a favorable result does not itself authorize a change of the canonical estimand. The common test cohort cannot establish scoring accuracy on possessions with substitutions.
