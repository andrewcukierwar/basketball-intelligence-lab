# DARKO / DPM — Future Research Program (R2)

**Status:** Placeholder for future primary-source research; **not implemented**.

Reference: https://www.darko.app/about

Research questions:
1. How are individual underlying player skill rates estimated dynamically over games and seasons?
2. What published Bayesian, Kalman/filtering, aging and regression elements can be reproduced faithfully?
3. How are statistical projections connected to adjusted on-court impact in Daily Plus-Minus?
4. How do model responsiveness and uncertainty compare with EPM's Estimated Skills and prior-informed RAPM?
5. Which details are undocumented and need explicit inference rather than invented facts?

**Replication rule:** Train only from basketball evidence and future outcomes, not DARKO/DPM rating labels. Preserve as-of data boundaries and publish independent holdouts.

**Start after:** R1 EPM baseline and shared data/evaluation infrastructure are reliable.
